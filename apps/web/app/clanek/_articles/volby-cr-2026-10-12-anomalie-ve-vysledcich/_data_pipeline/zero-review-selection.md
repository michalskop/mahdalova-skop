# Separate zero review

Source: frozen low-count audit `runs/20261011_1556_refresh/low_count_audit`. This export preserves all 191 already-flagged zeros. `priority_zeros.csv` must be reviewed alongside volume-deficit and low-nonzero-count selections when preparing an article.

Party size: small below 2%, middle from 2% to below 10%, big from 10%. These configurable thresholds are screening conventions, not calibrated error probabilities. Citywide shares exclude the target precinct. Expected local share uses held-out expected counts divided by the reported precinct total. Citywide party size and local expected support remain separate columns.

Priority zeros require an explicit zero, registered candidates, at least 50 valid ballots (or Senate votes), and middle or big expected local support. The source audit already requires at least 100 expected municipal candidate marks or 10 Senate votes in either the conditional-count expectation or the independent-volume expectation. The latter uses an independently predicted total utilisation rather than the reported precinct vote total. Ústí nad Labem 1001 and Plzeň 1005 qualify through the independent-volume expectation; their conditional expected counts are below 100. There is no 10% citywide gate, volume-deficit requirement, matching district candidacy requirement, repair-fit requirement, or mandate-impact requirement. A locally strong citywide small party can qualify; a citywide big party with tiny local expected support is kept in the other-review table. Small samples and missing rows also remain visible separately.

Keep `expectation_basis`, model support, independent-volume expectation, and completeness columns visible during review. Peer baselines are weaker than held-out political models. If no conditional local share can be computed, the row remains in the unknown-local-support channel even if an independent-volume prediction triggered the source audit. All unflagged zeros and zeros without expectations remain in the source audit's inventories.

Municipal candidate marks are not independent voters. This export does not calculate a binomial zero probability, infer a correct count, or establish a counting error. Historical geography and special electorates require separate review.

## Article supplement verification (14 October 2026)

Run `python validate_zero_supplement.py /path/to/runs/20261011_1556_refresh` after the original `build.py`. The validator checks the 78 unique council/physical-precinct keys, all 17 priority zeros, four overlaps and 13 additions; it preserves the original 65 rows and leaves new volume-deficit estimates blank. It compares the published Markdown table with the merged CSV, checks reported ballots and votes against the frozen primary ČSÚ XML batches, verifies party labels and valid candidate counts against the frozen ČSÚ registry, recomputes utilisation and the added rows' council medians, reproduces priority selection and local/citywide size classifications from the audit, and verifies the Brno-jih 8002 neighbours and model range. `zero-review-validation.json` records the results and hashes of the frozen inputs. Model expectations are checked against the frozen audit; this verification does not refit the political models.

The raw XML batches use the ČSÚ namespace `http://www.volby.cz/kv/` and the registry tables are `kvros.csv` and `kvrk.csv`. The official source and schema index is [ČSÚ municipal election open data, 2026](https://volby.gov.cz/opendata/kv2026/kv2026_opendata.htm); the article continues to use the frozen 11 October results rather than silently substituting later revisions. The archive paths below `sources/` and their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the validation JSON. Conditional expected counts are expected local shares multiplied by the reported precinct total; independent-volume counts additionally use expected overall utilisation. Neither count is an inferred correct result.
