## The Data Magician

**Michal Škop** is editor-in-chief and co-owner of DataTimes. Statistician, demographer and programmer, founder and director of the non-profit [Voting Advice Applications](https://volebnikalkulacka.cz/). Winner of the Czech Journalism Award (2025) with three shortlist nominations.

He has worked at the Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research in Rostock, the University of Salamanca and Charles University. He specialises in election analysis and prediction and teaches at the University of West Bohemia in Pilsen.
