DataTimes.eu is the European edition of DataTimes, the data journalism project of Kateřina Mahdalová and Michal Škop. We look at European questions through open data: elections, the rules that Brussels changes, and what those changes do to people, prices and markets.

We look for the facts and record them honestly. We don't tint them, we don't work for interest groups, and we are not afraid.

**Data. Context. Clarity. Courage.**

## Who we are

**Kateřina Mahdalová**, editor-in-chief and owner, is a journalist and analyst who led data projects at the Czech News Agency (ČTK) in 2016–2019. She is a two-time winner and eight-time finalist of the Czech Journalism Award, with a Data Journalism Award nomination.

**Michal Škop** is a statistician, demographer and programmer, founder and director of the non-profit [Voting Advice Applications](https://volebnikalkulacka.cz/), which since 2006 have helped millions of voters in many countries. He has worked at the Max Planck Institute in Rostock and at the University of Salamanca, and specialises in election analysis and forecasting.

More about us and our full archive, in Czech, is on [DataTimes.cz](https://datatimes.cz).
